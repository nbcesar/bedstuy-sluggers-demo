Our Mission & Story

Mentors On and Off the Field Since 2014

The mission of the Bed-Stuy Sluggers is to provide the youth of Bedford-Stuyvesant and surrounding Central Brooklyn neighborhoods an enjoyable, safe, team-oriented experience while learning the game of baseball. We set a common standard of helping our participants build character, leadership, accountability, communication skills, and a work ethic second to none.
